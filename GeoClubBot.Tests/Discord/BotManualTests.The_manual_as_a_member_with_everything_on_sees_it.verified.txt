# 🔗 Account linking
Link your Discord account to your GeoGuessr account; many features need it.

**`/gg-account link`** — Link your Discord account to your GeoGuessr account
- `share-profile-link` — Your GeoGuessr profile's share link, like https://www.geoguessr.com/user/62c353a2…
To get the link: GeoGuessr → **Profile** → the share button left of *EDIT AVATAR* → copy.

# ⏰ Daily reminder
A DM at times you choose, naming what you still owe today: your streak, a club mission.

### What it reminds you of
- **Your streak:** play the daily challenge or a duel (UTC day).
- **A club mission:** claim one while the board has a free mission and you haven't used today's claim. If you still hold a mission from an earlier day, it reminds you to finish it (or ask for help) instead.
The DM names only what is left, and none is sent once nothing is. Claims reset at 12:00 UK time; the reminder says when. Without a linked account I can't see your activity, so you are reminded of both.
You can have several reminders, each with its own time and message. If I was offline at a reminder's time, I send one catch-up DM when I'm back.

### Commands
**`/daily-reminder add`** — Add a daily reminder, or change the one at that time
- `time` — When, in 24-hour HH:mm, e.g. 09:00 or 21:30
- `timezone` *(optional)* — Your time zone, e.g. Europe/Berlin; UTC if left out
- `message` *(optional)* — Your own text; {{outstanding_text}} marks where what you still owe goes
Write `{{outstanding_text}}` in your message where what you still owe should go. It starts with a verb and ends the sentence, so `Time for GeoGuessr! Don't forget to {{outstanding_text}}` becomes
> Time for GeoGuessr! Don't forget to play the daily challenge (or a duel) and claim a club mission (5 still free on board 3, the daily claim resets in 4 hours)!
Without it, only your own text is sent.

**`/daily-reminder remove`** — Remove one of your daily reminders
- `reminder` — The reminder to remove, picked by its time

**`/daily-reminder clear`** — Remove all of your daily reminders

**`/daily-reminder list`** — List your daily reminders: times, time zones, messages and when each was last sent

# 📊 Your activity
Your streak, club missions and XP, and where you stand on the club's weekly requirements.

**`/my-activity current-week`** — Your streak, club missions and requirements since the last weekly check
The week the next check judges (it runs right after GeoGuessr's weekly board reset):
- XP earned, and your **rule XP**: what averages and swaps count, without the board-clear bonus and with at most 3 missions a week
- streak days and club missions finished (missions you claimed; helping doesn't count)
- the requirements and where you stand, e.g. `✅ streak 6/6 · ❌ missions 1/2`
- the missions of others you pressed **help out** on (for information only)
- a day-by-day strip: 🟩 streak kept, ⬛ not kept, a digit for missions finished that day
Needs your account linked.

**`/my-activity last-days`** — Your streak, club missions and XP over the last days
- `days` *(optional, default 7)* — How many days back to include
A rolling window instead of the check week, so no requirements. GeoGuessr's feed reaches back about two weeks. Needs your account linked.

# 🏆 Club stats
How the club is doing today, and this week's mission boards.

**`/club-stats todays-xp`** — A club's XP so far today, streaks kept, missions finished and claimed
- `club` *(optional)* — The club; the main club if left out
Today is the UTC day; claims count in the current claim cycle.

**`/club-stats board`** — This week's club mission boards and who holds which open mission
- `club` *(optional)* — The club; the main club if left out
When the week ends and claims reset, each board (✅ cleared, ▶️ current, 🔒 locked), and every open mission: who holds it, its progress, when it was claimed, and 🆘 if they asked for help.

**`/club-stats board-alerts`** — Get a DM when your club's next mission board is unlocked or a new week starts
- `alerts` — Every new board, only the next one, or off
So you can pick an easy mission before others take them. Your club is the one your linked GeoGuessr account is in, so link it first (`/gg-account link`).
Pick **every new board**, **only the next one** (the alerts then stop by themselves) or **off**. No DM is sent while you have already claimed this claim cycle, since you couldn't claim anyway. The 🔔 button under `/club-stats board` and the 🔕 button under each DM do the same.

# 🗺️ Country challenges
Themed country challenges on fixed days, with points for the top places and a leaderboard.

### How it works
On fixed days I post themed challenges, like *Mongolia Monday*, as GeoGuessr challenge links; finish all five rounds to count. A day or so later I post the results: the top places earn points (usually 3, 2 and 1) and sometimes a role. The points add up to a leaderboard I post regularly.

### Commands
**`/country-challenges leaderboard`** — The season's leaderboard and your own place on it
Ties share a place. Your place, and a winner's role, need your account linked.

# 🤖 AI assistant
Ask about GeoGuessr metas and I answer from community guides, with their pictures.

### Asking a question
No command needed: **@-mention me** with your question, e.g. `@GeoClubBot what do Ghanaian bollards look like?`. I answer in the channel, so everyone learns, cite the guides I used and attach their pictures. Anything from general knowledge instead starts with "Not from the guides:". A hard question can take up to a minute.
- **A screenshot:** attach it and ask about it.
- **A plant:** ask "what tree is this?" with a screenshot, ideally a close crop (several pictures of one plant work too). Say where it was taken ("just south of Boston", a country, a Maps link) and I weigh what grows there.
- **A place:** paste a Google Maps Street View link and ask what gives it away; I check which clues its view shows.

### Follow-ups
**Reply to my answer**; no mention needed. I remember the conversation and the guides I cited, so "show me [2] bigger" works. Each reply starts its own branch, so your follow-ups and someone else's never mix. A conversation ends after a day of quiet.

### Rating answers
Click 👍 or 👎 under an answer; click it again to take it back. Rated conversations are kept to improve the answers, unrated ones are deleted after a while.

### Commands
**`/ai status`** — Which AI models are available, how much is indexed, and today's allowance
The AI runs on a free daily allowance that resets at 00:00 UTC, with an hourly cap per person, so "out of requests" is expected rather than broken. **Picture links** says whether my linked pictures load.

**`/ai search`** — What the guide library holds on a topic, without asking a model
- `query` — What to look for
- `country` *(optional)* — Only results from one country
Handy for finding the guide itself, or checking whether I know anything about a topic.

**`/ai quiz`** — A guide picture with four answers to pick from; everyone guesses once and scores points
- `country` *(optional)* — Only pictures of one country, e.g. Vietnam: then it asks where in it, or what is typical
- `difficulty` *(optional)* — How hard; easy or medium if left out
- `lesser-known` *(optional, default off)* — Only clues PlonkIt does not have
Easy, medium and hard questions score 1, 2 and 3 points. The answer shows after a while, or when whoever started it reveals it.

**`/ai quiz-scores`** — The quiz leaderboard: points over all time and over the last days

**`/ai review`** — Review a game or a Street View place: the clues each round showed, and what to study
- `link` *(optional)* — A challenge, game or Google Maps Street View link; empty for the latest daily challenge
Round by round: the clues that could be seen and point to the country and region, and for a missed round the easiest clue that tells the right country from your guess. It ends with what to practise first, and each round's view links to Google Maps. A vision model checks what a round showed and can be wrong; the review says so.
- a **challenge** link: your game in it (needs your account linked); empty for the club's latest daily challenge you played
- a **game** link (`/game/…` or `/results/…`): anyone's finished game
- a **Street View** link: what gives that place away

**`/ai feedback`** — How people have rated my answers: totals, by model, and recent comments
- `days` *(optional, default 30)* — Days to count over; 0 counts everything

Right-click a message → Apps → **👍 Good AI answer** — Rate one of my answers as good and say why; the comment is optional.

Right-click a message → Apps → **👎 Bad AI answer** — Rate one of my answers as bad and say why; the comment is optional.
